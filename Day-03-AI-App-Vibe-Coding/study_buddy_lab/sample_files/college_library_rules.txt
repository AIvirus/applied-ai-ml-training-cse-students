Greenfield College Library Rules

Opening hours
The library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays. On Saturdays it is open from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays. During examination weeks the library stays open until 10:00 PM.

Borrowing books
Each student can borrow up to 4 books at a time. The normal borrowing period is 14 days. A book can be renewed two times if no other student has reserved it. Reference books, newspapers and old question papers cannot be taken out of the library.

Late fees
If a book is returned late, the fine is 5 rupees per day for each book. If a book is lost, the student must pay the price of the book plus a processing fee of 100 rupees. Students with unpaid fines cannot borrow new books.

Library card
Every student gets a library card with their college ID. The card must be shown at the entry gate. If the card is lost, a new card costs 50 rupees and is ready in 3 working days.

Digital library
Students can use e-books and research journals from home by logging in to the college portal. The digital library has more than 20,000 e-books. Printing in the library costs 2 rupees per page for black and white and 10 rupees per page for colour.

Behaviour
Silence must be kept in the reading hall. Mobile phones must be on silent mode. Food is not allowed inside, but drinking water in a closed bottle is allowed. Group study rooms can be booked at the front desk for up to 2 hours.
