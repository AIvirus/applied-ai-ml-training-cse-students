Healthy Study Habits for Students

Sleep
Students should sleep 7 to 9 hours every night. Sleep helps the brain store what you learned during the day. Studying all night before an exam usually lowers marks because the tired brain cannot remember well.

The Pomodoro method
The Pomodoro method means studying with full focus for 25 minutes and then taking a 5 minute break. After four such rounds, take a longer break of 15 to 30 minutes. This method helps you stay focused and avoid getting tired.

Active recall
Active recall means testing yourself instead of only reading again. Close the book and try to write or say what you remember. Practice questions and flash cards are good ways to use active recall.

Spaced repetition
Spaced repetition means revising a topic again after some days, for example after 1 day, then 3 days, then 7 days. Each revision makes the memory stronger.

Food and water
Drink enough water during the day. Eat fruits, vegetables and proteins. Too much sugar and junk food can make you feel tired.

Exercise
Even 20 minutes of walking or sports each day improves focus and mood. Exercise also helps you sleep better.

Phone and distractions
Keep your phone in another room or switch off notifications while studying. Every interruption can take several minutes to get your focus back.
