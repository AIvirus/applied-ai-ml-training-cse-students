Notes on the Solar System

The Sun
The Sun is a star at the centre of our solar system. It is a huge ball of hot gas, mostly hydrogen and helium. Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach the Earth.

The eight planets
In order from the Sun, the planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune. The first four are rocky planets. Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants. Uranus and Neptune are ice giants.

Interesting facts
Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest to the Sun. Venus is the hottest planet because its thick clouds trap heat. Mars is called the Red Planet because of iron oxide, which is rust, on its surface. Jupiter is the largest planet and has a giant storm called the Great Red Spot. Saturn is famous for its bright rings made of ice and rock. Neptune has the strongest winds in the solar system.

The Moon
The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It takes about 27 days to go around the Earth. The Moon causes ocean tides because of its gravity.

Dwarf planets
Pluto was called the ninth planet until 2006. In 2006 the International Astronomical Union changed its group to dwarf planet, because it has not cleared other objects from its path around the Sun.

Indian space missions
Chandrayaan-3 landed near the south pole of the Moon on 23 August 2023. India became the first country to land near the lunar south pole. Mangalyaan, also called the Mars Orbiter Mission, reached Mars orbit in 2014 on its first attempt.
